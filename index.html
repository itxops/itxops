FAQ

Questions We Get Asked.

If something here is not covered, email us and ask.

Do you work alongside an existing IT team?

Yes. We work either alongside an in-house team, handling the infrastructure and deployment layer, or as the technical function where there is not one.

Can you take over a server or site nobody is maintaining?

That is a common starting point. The first step is to understand what runs where and how it is configured, before anything gets changed.

Which technologies do you work with?

Mainly Linux servers, Apache and Nginx, Node.js, PHP, MySQL, WordPress, cPanel, Jenkins, DNS, and business email on Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 — across cloud and VPS environments.

Do you need access to our systems straight away?

No. Start by describing the problem. Access is arranged only once it is clear what is actually needed, and through whatever method you are comfortable with.

Can you migrate a website without downtime?

Migrations are planned so disruption is kept to a minimum — content and databases moved and tested first, DNS cut over last. How brief the switch is depends on the setup and DNS propagation.

Do you handle one-off problems as well as ongoing support?

Both. Some work is a single fix, deployment, or migration; some is continuous infrastructure support. Say which you are after and it can be scoped from there.

What if something breaks outside a planned change?

Email the same address and say it is urgent. Troubleshooting and restoring broken services are a core part of the work.

Do you document what you change?

Yes. Changes and configuration are recorded so the environment stays understandable — to you, and to anyone who works on it later.

Whether you’re dealing with a server issue, deployment problem, website migration, or business email setup — we’re here to help. Let’s discuss your requirements.
