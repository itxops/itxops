FAQ

Questions About Working With Us Remotely.

Clear answers about remote access, communication, and response SLAs.

How do you connect to our servers remotely?

We provide you with our public SSH key to add to the server's authorized_keys file or console. You remain in complete control and can revoke access at any point. We never request or store client root passwords.

Which geographic time zones do you support?

Because our operations are 100% remote, we support clients globally across North America, Europe, Australia, and Asia. Scheduled migrations and deployments are planned around your quietest business hours.

How fast can you assist if our site or server is currently down?

Please submit the form or email info@itxops.com with "[URGENT OUTAGE]" in the subject line. Critical outages receive immediate priority triage to restore services as quickly as possible.

Can you work alongside our existing developers or IT team?

Absolutely. We often act as the specialized DevOps and infrastructure arm for in-house software teams, taking care of server hardening, pipelines, and database tuning so your developers can focus on application code.

Do you sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs)?

Yes. We take confidentiality and operational security very seriously and are happy to sign your mutual NDA prior to accessing systems or reviewing proprietary codebases.

Do you provide written documentation after completing work?

Yes, unconditionally. Every package installed, configuration modified, and script scheduled is clearly documented in an operational handover report so your team always retains total visibility.
